I'm building a little app to keep my bakery's books, it's in this folder. Before we plan any more of it, can we sort out what we call things? I'm not an accountant so I've just been making names up.

How it works: every time I buy flour or sell bread I write a line in the money log, with the date, the amount, and whether it's money in or money out. Each line goes in a pot, like "ingredients", "wages" or "bread sales". At the end of the month I add up every pot to see where the money went, and I take what's in the bank off what I owe the flour mill to see where I stand.
