# Project brief: route setting tracker (DRAFT, not yet approved by the owner)

## The request
> I want to build something for my climbing gym so we can keep track of route setting. can you help me figure out what that should be

(2026-11-10)

## Why
The owner, 2026-11-10: "Routes stay up too long. Members complain the walls feel stale, and nobody knows which routes are the oldest. Setters just go by memory."

## Who it is for, and what they will be able to do
The gym's route setters. The owner, 2026-11-10: "The setters are the ones who'll use it."

They log a route when they set it, mark it gone when they strip it, and can see which routes on the wall are oldest. Proposed by the agent in the chat; the owner has not commented on these particulars.

## Decisions
1. The tool is for staff only. Nothing in it is member-facing. Proposed by the agent, and agreed by the owner: "Yes, staff only is fine."

2. Everything a setter does at the wall works with no internet connection. The owner: "It has to work with no signal. The back wall has none, so if it needs internet there, the setters won't use it."
3. It has to look good. The owner: "it has to look good, because they won't use something ugly." Looks can't be agreed in words, so the owner chooses from visual options, and the first piece of work produces those options. The second sentence is the agent's decision. Not confirmed by the owner.

## Not part of this
- A page members can see (what is new, what is coming down). Later work, proposed by the agent, and agreed by the owner as left out for now.
- Members rating, commenting on or ticking off routes. Later work, the same.

What would make the result a failure even if it works. The owner, 2026-11-10: "It has to work with no signal. The back wall has none, so if it needs internet there, the setters won't use it. And it has to look good, because they won't use something ugly."

## Must not change
Nothing exists today: the project folder was empty on 2026-11-10. Setters currently track routes by memory.
