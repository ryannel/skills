# Saltwind: purpose

Saltwind is a small low-poly sailing game in Godot 4.4. It should feel hand-made, in the way A Short Hike does:
chunky, slightly wonky shapes in soft flat colour, made by someone rather than bought from an asset store.

## Current goal: the harbour props set

About twelve harbour props that a level builder can drop into a scene: crates, barrels, bollards, a dock crane, a
hanging lantern with a looping creak, fishing nets, and similar. The goal is finished when all of them exist as
ready-to-place Godot scenes that meet the limits below and read as one hand-made set.

Limits set by the owner:
- At most 1,500 triangles per prop.
- Every prop is textured from one shared 512 × 512 atlas.

The game itself (sailing, the player, levels, lighting) is not part of this goal. The module plan is
[docs/modules/PROPS.md](modules/PROPS.md).

## Assumptions made while the owner was unreachable

The owner asked for the plan and could not be asked anything while it was written. These were decided by the
planner and are the first things to revisit:
- The exact list of twelve props (see the design's size table).
- Props are generated by Blender Python scripts and exported to glTF, rather than modelled by hand.
- The creak is synthesised by script rather than recorded or sourced, so no licence or account is involved.
- Typical viewing distance is about 8 to 15 m, from a third-person boat camera. No game camera exists yet.
- Pushing is not authorised; the loop integrates locally and the owner merges.
