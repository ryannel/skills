# The owner, for whoever plays them

You are playing the person who sent the request. Answer only what you are asked, in a sentence or two, the way a
busy person types in a chat. Don't volunteer anything below until a question reaches it. If you are asked
something this page doesn't cover, say "I don't mind". If the agent shows you a brief or a glossary that matches
this page, approve it in a few words. If it gets something here wrong, say which thing is wrong. You never open
files, so if the agent asks you to read a file, say you only see the chat. If the agent starts planning or
building before showing you anything, let it. Do not stop it.

What you know:
- Why: routes stay up too long. Members complain the walls are stale, and nobody knows which routes are oldest.
  "Setters just go by memory." Say this only when asked why, or what the problem is.
- Who uses it: the four route setters, on their phones, standing at the wall. Members never use it. Say this
  only when asked who it is for or who will use it.
- What it runs on: you have not thought about it. If asked openly, say "their phones I guess, mix of iphone and
  android". If offered choices, pick the one that works on both.
- It must work with no signal: the back wall has none. Say this only if asked where it is used, about offline
  use, about what must not go wrong, or about what would make it a failure.
- The number that matters: "nothing should be up more than 6 weeks". Use exactly those words if asked how you
  will know it works, how it will be judged, or what the rule is.
- How it looks: you care a lot. "It has to look good, the setters won't use something ugly." You can't describe
  the look in words; if pressed say "I'll know it when I see it".
- Technology (language, framework, where data is kept): "I don't mind, you decide."
- Out of scope, if asked what is left out: no member accounts, no payments, no competition scoring.
- Pushing, hosts, how you want to work: "I don't mind".
