## Positioning conflicts

**Two claimed owners:**

1. **"The default open video model."** `minimax-h3/SKILL.md:39` + `references/licence-and-territory.md:78` award it to Wan 2.2; `wan-2-2/SKILL.md:267` hands it to MiniMax H3 and keeps only "the Apache-2.0, controllable, trainable open video model". This is contradiction #1 above, but it is also the single positioning question a reader arrives with, so it deserves one settled sentence used identically in both skills. Recommended settlement: **H3 is the community default; Wan 2.2 is the suite's licence-clean default and the recommended starting point for anyone the other licences rule out.**

2. **Pose / depth / canny structural control.** `wan-2-2:162` claims sole ownership by denying LTX has a rig at all; `ltx-2-5:313` claims coverage while conceding depth. Contradiction #2 above. Settlement: Wan owns *camera* and *depth of rig*; LTX owns *reference-plus-mask spatial control on a 2.3-trained adapter set*. Both rows should say so.

**One job with no owner:**

3. **A freeform camera trajectory.** `wan-2-2:255` says Fun Camera does "explicit, discrete moves, **not** a freeform trajectory" and leaves its "Reach for instead" cell empty (`—`). LTX's camera-control LoRAs are LTX-2-era and `[flagged — re-verify]` (`ltx-2-5:52`); H3 is prompt-level only; SCAIL-2 inherits the driving clip's camera. So the suite's honest answer is "nobody, locally", and no skill says it. Recommend `wan-2-2:255` → "— nothing in the suite does a freeform camera path; the workaround is FLF2V or a driving clip through [`scail-2`](../scail-2/)."

4. **Local delivery above 1080p.** `minimax-h3:274` — 768p local ceiling, 2K hosted only. `ltx-2-5:169` — 4K only on the hosted Fast tier. Wan is 720p. Nobody states the suite-level conclusion (local ceiling is ~1080p; everything above is an upscale). Minor; a line in `image-production-workflows` would cover it rather than any one model skill.

**Verified single-owner, no conflict:** character replacement → `scail-2` (all four agree); native audio generate-*and*-consume → `ltx-2-5`; reference *voice* → `minimax-h3`; generative video re-render / finishing → `ltx-2-5`; LoRA training maturity → `wan-2-2`; lip-sync to a supplied track → `wan-2-2` S2V.
